Nathaniel Yoong

FPGA & Firmware Engineer · DSO National Laboratories

I build on Xilinx Zynq SoCs end to end: VHDL RTL in the programmable logic, IP integration in Vivado block designs, and bare-metal C on the Arm cores, with AXI DMA moving data between PS and PL. My focus is continuous, high-throughput data acquisition and DSP streaming.

2+ years full-time at DSO National Laboratories, after FPGA and hardware internships there since 2018. NTU EEE Honours (Distinction) and Temasek Polytechnic silver medallist. I'm most interested in FPGA acceleration and low-latency systems.

Where I've worked

All with the Guided Systems Division at DSO National Laboratories, from hardware testing intern to full-time engineer.

  1. Jul 2024 – Present

    Hardware & Firmware Engineer

    DSO National Laboratories · Guided Systems Division
    • Architected and implemented VHDL RTL for a continuous, high-throughput data acquisition and DSP streaming pipeline, using AXI4-Stream with buffering and backpressure to sustain throughput
    • Built AXI DMA data paths into DDR for real-time processing, alongside QSPI flash and eMMC storage
    • Closed timing on my own designs by reading static timing reports, tracing failing paths and reworking the RTL
    • Designed the schematic for a custom Zynq-7000 board with an onboard Ethernet PHY, brought it up and implemented bare-metal PS Ethernet at 100 Mbps
    • Debugged boards and designs with oscilloscope and JTAG; integrated FPGA, embedded C and PetaLinux with software and systems teams
    • Automated Vivado/Vitis builds with TCL, and helped pilot AI-assisted workflows for RTL design, debugging and documentation
