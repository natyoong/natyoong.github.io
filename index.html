Nathaniel Yoong

FPGA & Firmware Engineer · DSO National Laboratories · Singapore

I build on Xilinx Zynq SoCs end to end: VHDL RTL in the programmable logic, IP integration in Vivado block designs, and bare-metal C on the Arm cores, with AXI DMA moving data between PS and PL. My focus is continuous, high-throughput data acquisition and DSP streaming.

2+ years full-time at DSO National Laboratories, after FPGA and hardware internships there since 2018. NTU EEE Honours (Distinction) and Temasek Polytechnic silver medallist. I'm most interested in FPGA acceleration and low-latency systems.
